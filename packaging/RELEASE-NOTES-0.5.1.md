## What's New

- **Passwords in your keyring** — account passwords are now kept in your desktop keyring (GNOME Keyring, KWallet, KeePassXC) instead of the accounts file, and passwords you already saved move over automatically.
- **Verified downloads** — Proton versions and umu-launcher are checked against their published checksums before they are used; Proton releases that publish no checksum can no longer be installed.
- **Safer server lists and links** — server lists are only downloaded over HTTPS, and only web links are opened.
- **Private files** — the launcher's settings, logs and data are readable only by you, and passwords never appear in the log.
- **Cleaner packages** — the .deb now ships a man page and changelog and passes Debian's package checks.

Install: the tarball, the .deb, or `ac-launcher-bin` from the AUR.
